About us

Data-driven innovation for Oman's institutions and youth

Data Academy is a renowned institution specializing in training and digital content creation in the Sultanate of Oman. As a leading training and consulting establishment, we deliver holistic training and consulting solutions to both government and private organizations across domains such as technology, administration, education, and research.

We place particular emphasis on digital content creation, data analysis, and dynamic statistical reporting.

Our overarching objective: to execute initiatives and programs in the field of data science, while effectively integrating them with Fourth Industrial Revolution technologies.

Vision

Fostering innovation through utilizing future skills in the field of data science.

Mission

Cultivating knowledge and values associated with innovation by creating and delivering content related to data science and future skills to youth — everywhere.

Data Mining Training Program

A comprehensive course on harnessing the power of data for informed decision-making, helping organizations uncover valuable insights, patterns and trends hidden within vast datasets.

Technical 30%Strategic 70%
Program objectives
  • Identify the data-collection process and assess its validity for developing sustainable goals in decision-making.
  • Prepare data to align with visions, strategies, sustainable goals and decision-making processes.
  • Describe data in a way that enhances understanding and enables more strategic decisions.
  • Identify data-analysis mechanisms and data-science applications to understand trends and patterns, and provide strategic guidance based on results.
  • Write reports and make decisions that achieve the best results and data-driven strategic choices.
  • Create the journey of innovation in data and its products.

Data-Driven Leadership

Enables participants to master the skills required to harness the power of data and analytics for effective leadership decisions. The course explores the intersection of data analysis, leadership principles and business acumen, providing practical insight into leveraging data for strategic decision-making.

Database Management with Microsoft Access

From PowerPoint to Infographic

Designed to equip you with the skills and knowledge needed to craft visually engaging, informative PowerPoint presentations using infographic elements. The program combines 5% theory with 95% hands-on practice so participants leave with presentations that communicate information effectively.

Business professionalsEducatorsGraphic designersContent creatorsResearchers and analysts

  • Identify key data points and messages to convey.

  • Create visually appealing, cohesive slides using infographic techniques.
  • Apply colour, typography and layout principles to enhance visual communication.
  • Use storytelling to make presentations engaging and memorable.
  • Incorporate best practices for presenting and delivering infographics.
